
## 1. What was the vision of InsightForge at the start of Sprint 0?

The vision of InsightForge was to build a **production-grade AI research platform** that autonomously researches technical topics, synthesizes information from multiple sources, and generates structured, editable research reports.

The objective was not to build a simple AI chatbot or RAG demo, but to create a complete AI system that demonstrates modern software engineering practices, scalable architecture, and agentic workflows.

The project was also designed as a long-term learning project that would teach production software engineering while building a portfolio-quality application.

---

## 2. What problem was it intended to solve?

Researching technical topics often requires switching between multiple sources such as documentation, GitHub repositories, research papers, blog posts, and videos. Collecting, organizing, and summarizing this information manually is time-consuming.

InsightForge aims to solve this by:

- Automatically planning research tasks
- Gathering information from multiple sources
- Synthesizing findings
- Generating structured research reports
- Allowing users to edit and refine the generated reports

---

## 3. Who were the target users?

The primary target users are:

- AI Engineers
- Machine Learning Engineers
- Software Engineers
- Technical Researchers
- Students learning AI and software engineering

Future versions may also support engineering teams and organizations that perform technical research.

---

## 4. What was the long-term vision?

The long-term vision is to build a scalable AI research platform capable of:

- Autonomous multi-agent research
- Streaming research progress in real time
- Producing citation-backed reports
- Supporting editable research documents
- Orchestrating AI workflows using LangGraph
- Demonstrating production-grade backend architecture, frontend architecture, observability, testing, and deployment

From a personal perspective, the project also serves as a flagship portfolio demonstrating end-to-end AI system development.
